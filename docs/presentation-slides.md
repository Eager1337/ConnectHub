## Slide 2 — The problem
- Campus information can be spread across class groups, messages, notices and word of mouth.
- Students may miss activities or struggle to find relevant study communities.
- Design question: How might one clear interface make campus information easier to discover?

## Slide 3 — Brainstorming and selection
- ConnectHub — university community platform
- StudyMate — peer study matching
- QueueLess — campus service queue interface
- Selected: ConnectHub, because its core tasks are feasible to prototype and evaluate.

## Slide 4 — Goals and objectives
- Understand student needs through documented research.
- Organise feed, discovery, events, messages, saved posts and notifications.
- Build a responsive front-end prototype.
- Apply HCI principles and evaluate representative tasks.

## Slide 5 — Research methodology
- Proposed: 5–8 voluntary student participants.
- Short semi-structured interviews about campus information and collaboration.
- Task-based evaluation: find event, save post, publish update, find group, RSVP, mark notifications read.
- Record completion, errors, hesitation and comments. Report only genuine findings.

## Slide 6 — Prototype overview
- Home — feed and post composer
- Discover — student interest groups
- Campus Events — details and RSVP
- Messages — demonstration conversation list
- Saved Posts and Notifications

## Slide 7 — Demonstration
- Publish a sample update.
- Like and save a feed post.
- Open Saved Posts and retrieve the item.
- RSVP to a campus event.
- Mark sample notifications as read.

## Slide 8 — HCI principles
- Visibility of system status: active page and action feedback.
- Consistency: repeated cards, labels and controls.
- Recognition rather than recall: visible navigation.
- User control: reversible like and save actions.
- Responsive layout for smaller screens.

## Slide 9 — Evaluation and limitations
- Use task completion, errors and participant comments.
- Prototype uses sample data and local browser interactions.
- No real authentication, database, moderation or messaging backend.
- Testing and accessibility review remain necessary.

## Slide 10 — Conclusion
- ConnectHub brings campus information and community activities into one interface.
- The project demonstrates user-centred research, interface design and an evaluation plan.
